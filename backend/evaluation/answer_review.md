### en-pudhumai

**Q:** Pudhumai Penn for college students

The Pudhumai Penn scheme (Moovalur Ramamirtham Ammaiyar Higher Education Assurance Scheme) provides higher-education assistance for eligible girl students who studied in Tamil Nadu government schools and pursue a recognized higher-education course.

The Penkalvi portal is named as an application route for the Pudhumai Penn scheme.

Directory summaries like this are not current controlling Government Orders, and current rules and application status require confirmation from the Tamil Nadu Social Welfare and Women Empowerment Department.
