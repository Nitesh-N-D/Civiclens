# Initial user personas and journeys

**Status:** Hypotheses for discovery, not research-derived personas  
**Research date:** 9 November 2026

These provisional profiles guide interviews. They are not claims about every member of a demographic group. Validate or replace them with participant evidence before designing detailed workflows.

## Persona hypotheses

### 1. College student seeking education support

- **Goal:** Learn which state or central scholarship/education support may fit and what to do next.
- **Possible friction:** Multiple eligibility conditions, school-history requirements, income/community certificates, institution-specific steps, application windows.
- **Trust need:** Know whether guidance applies to their exact education stage and see the official notice and application portal.
- **Journey hypothesis:** Describe current study situation → see candidate schemes and exclusions → check conditions → view documents and deadlines → follow official portal or campus assistance route.
- **Do not assume:** All students have reliable private internet, bank access, or digitized documents.

### 2. Farmer or agricultural household member

- **Goal:** Find a relevant assistance or service and determine the local department/portal route.
- **Possible friction:** Scheme rules differ by crop, land/tenant status, district, season, and central/state administration; details may be updated through notices.
- **Trust need:** Current source and issuing department, clear caveats, offline contact route.
- **Journey hypothesis:** Ask in Tamil or mixed Tamil-English → narrow by high-level situation without collecting unnecessary identifiers → compare possible programs → confirm with source/department → proceed through official channel.
- **Do not assume:** Farming information needs are identical across districts or that a generic eligibility answer is safe.

### 3. Senior citizen or caregiver looking for pension support

- **Goal:** Understand the available pension/service path, conditions, documents, and where to get help.
- **Possible friction:** Similar scheme names, age/residency and household conditions, paperwork, uncertainty about online versus assisted application.
- **Trust need:** Simple steps, readable Tamil, a printable/shareable checklist, clear human contact and official source.
- **Journey hypothesis:** Select a topic → answer only necessary general questions → get a candidate pathway with conditions → review a short checklist → contact/visit the official service channel.
- **Do not assume:** The older adult is the device user or can complete a digital form independently.

### 4. Woman or family member looking for welfare assistance

- **Goal:** Determine whether a family or individual may qualify for a support program and how to apply.
- **Possible friction:** Rules split between departments, uncertainty about benefit amount, household circumstances, and required proof.
- **Trust need:** Non-judgmental language, privacy, no false promise of eligibility, explanation of exclusions.
- **Journey hypothesis:** Ask in preferred language → distinguish relevant scheme category → review conditions and evidence → understand unresolved details → take the official next step.
- **Do not assume:** A chatbot is a safe place to disclose personal or sensitive information.

### 5. Small business owner seeking public support

- **Goal:** Find schemes or services for registration, credit, subsidy, training, or compliance.
- **Possible friction:** Different programs define enterprise type, registration, geography, sector, and application route differently.
- **Trust need:** Department source, current terms, distinction between assistance and loans, explicit application destination.
- **Journey hypothesis:** Describe business type and location → compare possible programs → see required registration and documents → check official source → proceed or consult a verified service intermediary.
- **Do not assume:** Search results from private consultants are authoritative or cost-free.

### 6. Community helper / e-Sevai or NGO volunteer

- **Goal:** Explain public information to a client quickly and consistently.
- **Possible friction:** Repeating searches, outdated screenshots, hard-to-share source material, language mismatch.
- **Trust need:** Copyable citations, date checked, a concise checklist, clear escalation when content is uncertain.
- **Journey hypothesis:** Search on behalf of a citizen → show sourced explanation → verify the official page together → share next steps without storing the citizen's personal data.
- **Do not assume:** This persona has authorization to enter or retain client data.

## Shared journey to test

1. State a need in Tamil, English, or mixed language.
2. Clarify only the minimum facts needed for useful filtering.
3. Review zero or more candidate services, with “not enough evidence” as a valid outcome.
4. Understand each condition in plain language, including exclusions and uncertainty.
5. See the official source, relevant evidence, owner, and last verification date.
6. Get a checklist and official online or assisted next step.

Interviewers should record where this journey differs from reality. Avoid treating proposed personas or AI features as validated user requirements.
