# Social-impact measurement plan

**Status:** Proposed measures; no baseline or impact result exists  
**Geography:** Tamil Nadu initial pilot

Measure whether the product helps people reach and understand authoritative information. Site visits, AI usage, or number of schemes indexed are not by themselves evidence of public benefit.

| Outcome | Operational measure | Data-minimizing collection | Interpretation limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Faster information discovery | Median task time to find the correct official source, compared with participant's current method | Anonymous timed usability tasks with fictional scenarios | Faster is not better if source choice or understanding is wrong. |
| Better comprehension | Share of participants who correctly explain a rule, caveat, or unknown after reading | Short, anonymous answer-key task | Small pilot findings are directional, not population estimates. |
| Reliable official handoff | Share of tasks reaching the correct official application/service route | Link-choice observation; do not track users across external government portals | Clicking a link does not mean the application was submitted or benefit received. |
| Safer eligibility explanation | Unsupported claims, citation errors, false-eligibility interpretations, and appropriate abstentions per reviewed test question | Human-reviewed bilingual evaluation set; no real profiles | Low test error does not guarantee safety for unseen schemes or people. |
| Tamil accessibility | Task completion and comprehension in Tamil compared with English, alongside user preference | Anonymous task-level language choice and short feedback | Language choice is contextual; do not infer ability or identity. |
| Inclusive assisted access | Appropriate referral to e-Sevai/college/department help when digital self-service is unsuitable | Count task-level referrals in synthetic/consented sessions | A referral is not proof the offline service was accessible or completed. |
| Trust calibration | Share understanding CivicLens is independent and does not determine eligibility | One comprehension question after a task | Trust should not be maximized if it creates over-reliance. |

## Reporting rules

- Publish baseline, denominator, collection period, language, and limitations with every metric.
- Separate observed task outcomes from self-reported confidence and from real benefit receipt.
- Do not claim scheme enrollment, money saved, or benefits received unless measured with informed consent and an appropriate lawful method.
- Keep research notes de-identified; suppress small-group breakdowns that could identify a participant.
- Use unresolved requests and abstentions to guide source research rather than prompting the model to guess.

No impact metrics have been collected.
